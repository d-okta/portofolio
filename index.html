Saya pelajar kelas 12 Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak di SMK Negeri 10 Surabaya dan akan lulus tahun ini. I'm a final-year Software Engineering student at SMK Negeri 10 Surabaya, graduating this year. I've built websites and apps for real clients, mostly on my own. Outside code, I work in broadcasting as an editor and creative team coordinator.
